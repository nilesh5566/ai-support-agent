# Internal Support Policies (staff only)

## Escalation matrix
Safety reports (smoke, burning smell, sparks, carbon monoxide) are urgent and go to the Safety team within 15 minutes.
Legal threats, chargebacks and data-protection requests go to Trust & Safety with urgent priority.
Enterprise customers with a site outage are escalated to the account manager with high priority.

## Refund approval limits
Tier 1 agents may approve refunds up to $100 without approval.
Refunds between $100 and $500 require a team lead. Refunds above $500 require the support manager.

## Goodwill credits
Agents may offer one goodwill credit of one free month of Pro per customer per year after a confirmed service incident.
Do not offer goodwill credits for issues caused by customer wiring mistakes.

## Known issue KI-213
Thermostat Gen 2 firmware 4.2.1 can lose Wi-Fi after router reboots. Fix ships in 4.2.3.
Workaround: restart the thermostat after the router is back online. Do not RMA devices for this issue.
