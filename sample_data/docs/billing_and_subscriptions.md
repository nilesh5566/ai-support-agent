# Billing and Subscriptions

## Plans
Acme Cloud has three plans. Starter is free and includes live control and 7 days of history.
Pro costs $5.99 per month or $59 per year and adds 1 year of energy history, geofencing and priority email support.
Enterprise is priced per site and includes SSO, an account manager and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Changing or cancelling your plan
You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time in the app under Profile > Subscription.
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Refunds
Monthly Pro subscriptions are not refundable for partial months.
Annual Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase or renewal.
After 14 days, annual plans can be refunded on a prorated basis only if a service outage affected the account.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Payment methods and failed payments
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal.
If a renewal payment fails, we retry three times over 7 days and your account status becomes past due.
Update your card in Profile > Subscription > Payment method to resolve a past due status.
Accounts that stay past due for 14 days are moved to the Starter plan; no data is deleted.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed after each payment and are available in Profile > Subscription > Invoices.
Enterprise customers can request invoices with a purchase order number.
