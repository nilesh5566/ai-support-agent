# Acme Smart Home Product Guide

## Getting started with the Acme Thermostat
Download the Acme Home app from the App Store or Google Play and create an account.
Tap Add Device, choose Thermostat, and scan the QR code on the back of the faceplate.
The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported.
Installation takes about 20 minutes. Always switch off power at the breaker before wiring.

## Resetting the thermostat
To restart the thermostat, press and hold the dial for 10 seconds until the screen turns off, then release.
To factory reset the thermostat, open Settings > Device > Factory Reset on the thermostat itself and confirm twice.
A factory reset erases schedules and Wi-Fi settings; the device must be added to the app again afterwards.

## Wi-Fi troubleshooting
If the device shows "Offline" in the app, first confirm your router is broadcasting a 2.4 GHz network.
Move the router or add a mesh node if signal strength in Settings > Network is below two bars.
Restart the router, then restart the device. Most connection problems are solved by these two steps.
If the device still will not connect, check that the Wi-Fi password contains no emoji characters.

## Room Sensors
Room Sensors measure temperature and occupancy and pair with the thermostat over Bluetooth Low Energy.
Each thermostat supports up to 6 Room Sensors. Sensor batteries (CR2032) last about 2 years.
To pair a sensor, pull the battery tab and hold it within 3 metres of the thermostat for 30 seconds.

## Smart Plugs
Smart Plugs support up to 15 amps and can be scheduled from the app or by voice assistant.
Plugs must not be used with space heaters or medical equipment.

## Acme Hub Pro
The Hub Pro connects up to 200 devices and supports Zigbee, Thread and Matter.
Hub Pro firmware updates install automatically at 3:00 AM local time.

## Two-factor authentication
You can enable two-factor authentication in the app under Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication.
We support authenticator apps and SMS codes. Save your backup codes somewhere safe.
