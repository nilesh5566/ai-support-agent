# Shipping, Returns and Warranty

## Shipping
Orders ship from our warehouse within 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99.
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Order status can also be checked by our support agent.

## Returns
Hardware can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it is in its original packaging.
Start a return by contacting support; we email a prepaid return label.
Refunds for returned hardware are issued within 5 business days after the item arrives at our warehouse.

## Warranty
All Acme hardware has a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
Warranty replacements require the order number and a short description or video of the defect.
Damage from incorrect wiring, water or power surges is not covered by the warranty.
